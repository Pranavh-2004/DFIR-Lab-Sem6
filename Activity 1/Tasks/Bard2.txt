Be not afraid. The island overflows with sound,
With gentle music and soft voices that bring joy and no harm.
At times a thousand ringing instruments
Drift through my ears, and at other times, voices
So soothing that, if I had just awoken from deep sleep,
They would lull me back into rest. And as I dreamed,
The clouds seemed to part and reveal great treasures
Poised to fall before me, so that when I awoke,
I longed to return to dreaming once more.
